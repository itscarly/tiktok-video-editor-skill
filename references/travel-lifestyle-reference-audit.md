## Shared rules to carry forward

- Start with the strongest visual in the first two seconds, then let the chosen format determine pace.
- Build a visible journey: context, movement, destination or task, payoff or return. Do not add clips only to reach a length target.
- Use hard cuts at natural motion, changes in direction, or music phrases. Reserve transitions for a real change of time or place.
- Use readable high-contrast text; keep it within safe vertical space, away from TikTok UI. One type treatment per post.
- Pick a fresh track per export. Match its energy to the chosen format; do not reuse prior music by default.
- Preserve POV privacy standards: remove phone, hands, keys, personal screens, and other identifiers when requested.
